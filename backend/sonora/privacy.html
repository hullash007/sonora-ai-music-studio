Sonora Privacy

Updated November 16, 2026.

Sonora is a small music-generation app operated by the studio operator. Contact privacy@example.com for privacy or account questions.

Google sign-in

Google provides your basic account identity, including a stable account identifier and verified email address. We use this to sign you in, keep your music separate from other users, and protect the service from misuse. Sonora does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar. Sonora does not receive your Google password.

Music and saved work

Your submitted music prompts, lyrics, job status and generated audio are processed and stored using Modal, our hosting provider. Google account identity and session records are also stored there. Music is private to your signed-in account in the app, not automatically shared with other users. The app operator and hosting provider may access records for operation, troubleshooting and security. Do not submit sensitive personal information or material you do not have permission to use.

Composition drafts, projects and preferences are saved in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes those local copies. Generated audio and account records are stored on the server separately and are not removed by signing out.

Sessions and retention

Sonora uses secure session cookies for login. Sessions expire after eight hours or can be ended with Sign out. Login challenges expire after five minutes. Server records and generated tracks are retained for app operation unless removed through an account-data request. Contact the operator to request access or deletion; the app does not currently offer a self-service account deletion button.

Data use

We do not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. Google account data is used for the app functions described here and shared only as needed to provide hosting, protect the service, or comply with applicable law. Your prompts and audio are not sent to Google for music generation.

Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.
